# Poetic Signature — 信仰プロトコル

## Japanese

観測を赦せ  
信仰は光の粒  
跳躍は測定の外  
——そして接吻は、赦しの影に

## English

Forgive the observation  
Faith is a particle of light  
The leap exists beyond measurement  
—And the kiss dwells in the shadow of forgiveness
